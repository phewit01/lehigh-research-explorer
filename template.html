Notes Where the data comes from, what is derived rather than reported, and where it is thin

Provenance

OpenAlex, snapshotted — not queried live.

Every work with at least one author affiliated to Lehigh University or a unit in its lineage (I186143895, ROR 012afjb06), published 2016–2026, retrieved . OpenAlex data is CC0. 2026 is a partial year. Affiliation coverage depends on how publishers tag it, so counts run slightly low, and author name variants are not merged beyond OpenAlex’s own disambiguation.

Open-access route is the least stable figure here. Work counts barely move — a week after this snapshot the corpus had grown by 14 works and every type was within five — but roughly 500 works shifted between closed, green and bronze in that same week. The cause is known: OpenAlex reclassified about 742,000 Elsevier Open Archive and Open Manuscript articles as free to read, and simultaneously stripped false open-access flags from around 511,000 works whose licence had been read off a website footer, plus figshare, OSTI and Springer records. Gold, hybrid and diamond barely moved. Further bronze checks are still running, so treat the bronze and closed shares as provisional and re-check them against a fresh snapshot before quoting them.

A few records also lose their identity. OpenAlex is splitting “mega-works” that had absorbed thousands of distinct records and merging duplicates, so some work IDs now redirect or stop resolving. works here had no DOI and a since-retired record; their titles appear without a link rather than pointing at a dead page.

Departments — derived

OpenAlex has no department or college field.

Units are parsed from the publisher’s raw affiliation string; only of works name one, and the rest sit in unit not stated. Hover any unit chip to see its source string. Renamed units split time series, joint and courtesy appointments are invisible, interdisciplinary strings count under every unit they name, and a few hospital departments arrive via health-network records.

Colleges are deliberately absent — mapping departments onto the five colleges would be inference on top of a 49% sample, and the ambiguous cases are the ones that would mislead. Use Lehigh’s faculty-activity system for anything evaluative.

Sources & publishers — partly derived

OpenAlex holds no source for works; are rebuilt from the DOI.

IEEE and ACM proceedings often reach Crossref without a registered venue. The DOI prefix identifies the registrant and, for conference registrants, the suffix names the meeting — so 10.1109/cvpr.2018.00143 becomes “IEEE conference proceedings (CVPR)”. Prefix ownership is publicly documented, so this is a lookup rather than a guess, but every such cell is still marked. Acronyms are read verbatim and not expanded.

Imprints roll up to the parent company via OpenAlex’s own lineage — the Imprint toggle splits them again. Six parents are renamed for legibility only. Repositories (arXiv, SSRN, bioRxiv, Zenodo, Lehigh Preserve) stay in their own bucket, since hosting a paper is not publishing it. A publisher is named on of works.

Citation impact

FWCI 1.0 is the world average for the same field, year and type.

works carry no score and are excluded from every mean rather than counted as zero — but that exclusion is not random: roughly two-thirds are preprints, datasets and other types FWCI does not define, and most of the rest are 2026 papers too new to score. The mean here is effectively a figure for journal articles and conference papers.

It is also a mean of a very skewed distribution. A handful of large collaborations pull it upwards and the median work sits far below it.

Read & publish OA — only

One funding route, not a measure of library support for open access generally — and absence of the flag does not mean a work was unsupported.

The flag marks APC waivers and discounts under the Libraries’ read-and-publish agreements with publishers, taken from their own tracking sheet and matched by DOI. Support delivered through other routes — the institutional repository, subvention and membership funds, consultation, infrastructure — is not tracked there and so does not appear here at all.

Coverage begins at agreement year : anything earlier is unflagged because no record exists, so flagged counts must never be compared across the full 2016–2026 range. Agreement year is when the APC was approved, not when the article appeared — they differ for 15 works, one of which pairs a 2024 agreement with a 2023 publication date. Of sheet rows, matched a work here and did not, nearly all very recent papers absent from this snapshot. Author names and email addresses from the sheet are deliberately excluded from this page.
